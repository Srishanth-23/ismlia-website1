Preamble & Scope

Industrial Applications of Machine Learning

Machine Learning (ML), an algorithmic approach to extracting meaningful information from data, is finding its way into an expanding range of fields, thanks to the power of modern-day computers in efficiently handling and processing vast amounts of data.

Although ML applications are ubiquitous in social networks, business, medical, and financial data analysis, it is beginning to establish itself as an inevitable tool to address and solve complex problems in industries. With the automation of industrial operations, capturing and storing of sensor measurement data has become relatively easier. As a result of this, many industries have voluminous data that can be processed to get a deeper insight into plant conditions and effectively used to improve the quality of operation.

It is in this context that industries are beginning to look at ML as a potential tool to address complex problems that conventional methods have failed to solve. This International Symposium ISMLIA 2026 provides a platform for participants from industry, academia, and the research community to get a significant overview of potential areas for industrial applications of ML algorithms.

THRUST AREA 01

Digital Twin

Combining first-principles physics models with data-driven ML algorithms for grey-box digital twins and real-time operational excellence.

THRUST AREA 02

Symbolic Regression

Discovering explicit mathematical expressions directly from sensor measurements to model nonlinear industrial dynamics.

THRUST AREA 03

Physics-Inspired Neural Networks

Incorporating physics knowledge and mechanistic constraints directly into AI knowledge representation systems.

THRUST AREA 04

Agentic AI

Autonomous multi-agent architectures for real-time value-driven implementation, process diagnosis, and control.
