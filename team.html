Eminent Speakers

Keynote Lecturers & Talk Summaries

Distinguished experts sharing practical insights on using ML to solve complex industrial problems.

Lecture I

Dr. Vijaysai Prasad

JGM & Head, Water Technology Centre, L&T Constructions, Chennai, India

Talk Title: Operational Excellence in Water Industry through Grey Box Digital Twins

Biography: Dr. Vijaysai Prasad (VJ) heads WTC Kanchipuram at L&T's WET business. With nearly three decades of experience in industrial research, he holds a Ph.D. from IIT Bombay, served at GE Global Research Center, GE Water, and SUEZ Water Technologies. He serves on jury committees for DST desalination proposals and Steering Committees for IWA & Digital Water India.

Talk Summary: Presents the principles and implementation of grey-box digital twins, combining first-principles physics models with data-driven AI/ML, using a reverse osmosis (RO) desalination plant as a real industrial case study.

Lecture II

Dr. Kanchi Lakshmi Kiran

Senior Vice President, Regional Business Analytics, DBS, Singapore

Talk Title: AI Transformation Era: Role of Agentic AI and its role of Real-Time Value-Driven implementation in Industrial Applications

Biography: Technology leader with 16 years of experience across AI, big data, and engineering. Currently leads Agentic AI at DBS, Singapore. Previously Lead/Senior Data Scientist at McLaren Applied Technologies and Senior Research Engineer at Yokogawa. Holds 4 international patents and a Ph.D. from NUS.

Talk Summary: Explores the shift to autonomous goal-directed Agentic AI, multi-agent frameworks, operational safety guardrails, shadow rollouts, and real-time value-driven implementations across energy, smart manufacturing, and finance.

Lecture III

Dr. Raghuraj K Rao

Managing Director and Head Technical Services, AKXA TECH Pvt. Ltd, Kolhapur, India

Talk Title: Multi Agent Systems for Process Diagnosis and Control

Biography: Chemical Engineer (BE: NITK Surathkal, M.Tech: IIT Bombay, Ph.D.: NUS Singapore) with over 25 years of academic, research, and commissioning experience. Founder Director & MD of AKXA Tech, delivering algorithm-based analytics tools for manufacturing stability and energy efficiency.

Talk Summary: Provides a bird's-eye view on operational excellence, process fault diagnosis, prediction, and real-time process optimization using Agentic Multi-Agent Systems in Industry 4.0 setups.

Lecture IV

Prof. Wu Zhe

Assistant Professor, Department of Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering, National University of Singapore (NUS)

Talk Title: Machine Learning in Model Predictive Control: Theoretical and Practical Challenges

Biography: Ph.D. from UCLA (2020), postdoctoral researcher at UCLA Computer Science. Published over 50 peer-reviewed articles, recipient of NUS Outstanding Early Career Award (2024), AIChE Singapore Young Faculty Award (2024), and Stanford Top 2% Scientist (2024, 2025).

Talk Summary: Presents a general framework of using Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN) for modeling nonlinear dynamic systems within Model Predictive Control (MPC), addressing data scarcity via physics-informed ML and transfer learning.

Lecture V

Prof. Ravindra Gudi

AI and ML Chair Professor & Deputy Director, IIT Bombay, India

Talk Title: Incorporating Physics in AI based Knowledge Representation Systems

Biography: Deputy Director (Provost equivalent) at IIT Bombay. Former Dean (Alumni & Corporate Relations) and Head of Chemical Engineering. Fellow of INAE, NASI, and IIChE. Author of 200+ Scopus papers and 11 US patents.

Talk Summary: Explores complementing data-driven AI/ML approaches with physics/mechanistic knowledge representation systems to improve model credibility, accuracy, and industrial decision-making.
