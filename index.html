Key Thrust Areas

Machine Learning in Industry 4.0

Pioneering applications in process modelling, digital twins, symbolic regression, and autonomous AI systems.

THRUST AREA 01

Digital Twin

Combining first-principles physics models with data-driven ML algorithms for grey-box digital twins and real-time operational excellence.

THRUST AREA 02

Symbolic Regression

Discovering explicit mathematical expressions directly from sensor measurements to model nonlinear industrial dynamics.

THRUST AREA 03

Physics-Inspired Neural Networks

THRUST AREA 04

Agentic AI

Autonomous multi-agent architectures for real-time value-driven implementation, process diagnosis, and control.
